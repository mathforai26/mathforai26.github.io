# Course schedule

The titles name the broader topic in each lecture, and the questions identify
the issue that organizes it. The 26-topic arc runs from September 1 through
December 3. Fall Reading Week is November 9--13, with no classes. The final
meeting on December 8 is reserved for synthesis, overflow, and oral-exam
preparation. Readings and precise theorem selections will be added later.
