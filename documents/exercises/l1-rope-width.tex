The following tool may be used in \cref{ex:rope-width}.
\begin{theorem}[Hoeffding's inequality]
\label{thm:hoeffding}
Let $n\ge1$ be an integer and let $Z_1,\ldots,Z_n$ be independent real
random variables. Suppose $Z_r\in[a_r,b_r]$ almost surely for each $r\in[n]$,
where $a_r<b_r$ are real numbers. For every real $t\ge0$,
\[
\mathbb P\!\left(\sum_{r=1}^n (Z_r-\mathbb E[Z_r])\ge t\right)
\le \exp\!\left(-\frac{2t^2}{\sum_{r=1}^n(b_r-a_r)^2}\right).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{exercisechunk}
\item \textbf{Reliable RoPE addressing at a fixed score scale.}
\label[exercise]{ex:rope-width}
\exerciseprofile{4}{3}{4}{\exproof\quad\excalculation\quad\exconstruction\quad\exinterpretation}{%
Prove matching logarithmic lower and upper bounds on the width needed for
uniform relative addressing with bounded coordinates.}
Fix an integer context length $T\ge2$ and use $S_i=[i]$. Determine, up to
constant factors, how many coordinates are necessary and sufficient to give
the requested source attention weight at least $3/4$, simultaneously for every
integer offset $1\le d<T$ and query position $i>d$.

Let $m$ be the number of coordinate pairs. In pair $r\in[m]$, use a fixed
unit key $k_r\in\mathbb R^2$, a frequency $\omega_r\in[0,2\pi)$, and the unit
query $q_r(d)$ from \cref{ex:rope}. With score scale $\lambda=1$, define
\[
s_{ij}=\sum_{r=1}^m
(R_{i\omega_r}q_r(d))^\top(R_{j\omega_r}k_r).
\]
\begin{enumerate}[(a),beginpenalty=10000]
\item Show that all scores lie in $[-m,m]$. Use
\cref{ex:bounded-attention} to prove that attention weight at least $3/4$ on
the requested source at $i=T$ requires
\[
m\ge\tfrac12\log\bigl(3(T-1)\bigr).
\]
\item Prove that, for every integer $m\ge8\log(2T)$, there exist frequencies
$\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_m$ such that
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:rope-separated-addresses}
\sum_{r=1}^m\cos(\omega_r h)\le m/2
\quad\text{for every integer }h\text{ with }1\le|h|<T.
\end{equation}
\item Fix frequencies satisfying \cref{eq:rope-separated-addresses}. Show
that the requested source has score $m$ and every other available source has
score at most $m/2$, simultaneously for all offsets and query positions.
Prove that the total attention weight outside the requested source is at most
$(T-1)e^{-m/2}$. Deduce that $m=\lceil8\log(2T)\rceil$ suffices for target
weight at least $3/4$. State the error bound when every value has norm at most
$B$, and conclude that $\Theta(\!\log T)$ coordinate pairs are necessary and
sufficient.
\end{enumerate}

With suitably chosen frequencies, RoPE therefore supports relative addressing
with logarithmic width and bounded coordinates. The coordinate pairs both
distinguish addresses and accumulate the score advantage needed to concentrate
attention. Width provides an alternative to increasing the score scale.

Standard RoPE uses geometrically spaced frequencies. Its frequencies satisfy
$0<\omega_r\le1$, so at displacement $h=1$,
\[
\sum_{r=1}^m\cos\omega_r\ge m\cos1>m/2.
\]
Thus the standard schedule fails the sufficient separation condition in
part (b); this does not rule out retrieval with that schedule.

\begin{hints}
For (a), apply \cref{ex:bounded-attention} with $T$ sources and score range
$2m$. For (b), choose the frequencies independently and uniformly on
$[0,2\pi)$. Compute $\mathbb E[\cos(\omega_r h)]$ for nonzero integer $h$,
then apply \cref{thm:hoeffding} and a union bound over $h\in[T-1]$. Use the
evenness of cosine for negative $h$.
\end{hints}
\end{exercisechunk}
